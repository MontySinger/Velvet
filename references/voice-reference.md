# Private voice calibration template

Keep the completed version in your own private workspace. Do not commit private writing samples, client details or personal correspondence to a public repository.

- Writer and communication mode:
- Audience and relationship:
- Source examples: author, date if known, provenance and relevance.
- Observed characteristic: evidence → tentative preference → scope.
- Explicit preferences: instruction, source, date and scope.
- Collaboration: materials, critique, targeted edits or full drafts.
- Task scene: situation, verified facts, intended emotion and action.
- Formats not yet calibrated:
- Approved correction: before/after, general or local scope, source and date.

Use a few matched examples as a working set. Separate your writing from quoted replies, transcripts of other speakers and generated drafts. Treat AI-assisted examples as deliberately selected style targets rather than evidence of unaided habits. Do not infer approval from silence.
